# Character Profile Photo

Your character's profile photo is their visual representation and the first impression they make on users. There are two ways to create an avatar: AI generation or uploading an image directly.

## AI Generated Profile Photo

Follow these steps to create a profile photo using GirlfriendPT's AI generation:

### Write the Character Description

Provide a detailed description of your character. This helps the AI generate a more accurate profile photo. You can write this description yourself or use GirlfriendPT's AI to generate a draft, which you can then edit to your liking.

💡 **Prompt Tips:**
- **Be Specific**: Clearly describe the character's appearance, including physical features, clothing, and accessories. The more details, the better.
- **Include Context**: Provide context for the scene or setting to help the AI generate a cohesive image.
- **Define the Mood**: Specify the mood or atmosphere you want to convey in the image.
- **Describe Actions or Poses**: If your character is engaged in a specific action or pose, describe it clearly.
- **Specify Colors and Textures**: Mention specific colors and textures to ensure the visual elements match your vision.

### Choose a Style

Select a style from GirlfriendPT's 3 available image styles. The styles include photography, realistic and anime. The AI will generate images based on your choice.

GirlfriendPT will generate 6 profile photos for you to choose from. Review the images and select the one that best represents your character. If you're not satisfied with any of the AI-generated images, you can upload your own picture to use as the profile photo.

## Direct Image Upload

If you prefer to upload an image directly:

### Select and Upload the Image

Choose a high-quality image that represents your character well. Ensure the image is clear and visually appealing.

### Crop the Image

You can crop the uploaded images as you like to meet your different needs for profile photos, chat backgrounds, and profile covers.

⚠️ **Important**: The images you upload will be run through moderation filters, make sure the images don't violate our guidelines to avoid failure setting.
