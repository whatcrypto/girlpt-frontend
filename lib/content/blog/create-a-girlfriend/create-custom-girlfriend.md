## Character Profile

The character profile provides essential information and context, shaping how users interact with your character.

### Name (Required)
The character's name, which is crucial for recognition and interaction. Ensure it's unique and relevant to the character.

### Identity (Required)
Describe who the character is briefly.

### Voice (Required)
A distinct voice enhances your character's presence and engagement. Choose a voice that matches your character's persona from GirlfriendPT's extensive library.

### Tag (Optional)
Choose tags to categorize your character for easier discovery.

### Bio (Optional)
Write a bio for your character that highlights the character's identity or personality. It should emphasize unique traits and create intrigue.

![Character Profile Setup](QuickCreation7)

### Description (Required)
Describe your character. You can write it from any aspects like personalities, background stories, relationships, internal thoughts, memory, and so on.

### Greeting (Required)
Write the first few words the character sends. The first sentence users will see from your character, setting the tone for the interaction. A good greeting will reflect the character's personality and set the scene.

### Conversational Style (Optional)
You can train your character's dialogue through conversational style.

![Character Configuration](QuickCreation8)

### Visibility (Required)
This setting defines who will be able to talk to your character.

- **Public**: The character will be visible on explore. If you change visibility into "private" or "unlisted", those who have already chatted can continue conversing with the character.
- **Unlisted**: Anyone with the link you share can see and chat with your character.
- **Private**: Only you can see and chat with the character.
